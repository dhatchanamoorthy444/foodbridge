---

## Tone

Clean, bright aesthetic with bold, friendly typography and vibrant imagery. The layout is organized into distinct, visually separated sections, creating a welcoming and approachable feel. Feels like a curated collection of offerings.

Food branding, bright design, food photography, family dining, approachable restaurant feel.

## Typography

- **Display/Headlines:** Bold, friendly, high contrast
- **Body:** Clear, readable on light backgrounds
- **Line-height:** Generous spacing for approachability

---

## Spacing

Consistent, grid-based spacing with clear visual breathing room between sections.

---

## Components

- **Hero with CTA:** Large imagery + action button
- **Feature Trio:** 3-column feature blocks
- **Logo Cloud:** Partner/supporter grid
- **Food Photography:** High-quality imagery throughout

---

## Layout Principles

1. **Feature Stack:** Sequential sections stacked vertically
2. **Visual Hierarchy:** Bold typography + vibrant imagery
3. **Whitespace:** Generous margins and breathing room
4. **Call-to-Action:** Clear, prominent buttons throughout
5. **Color Contrast:** Red/pink on white for high visibility

---

## FoodBridge Adaptation Notes

Apply this to create:
- Light, welcoming homepage
- Clear feature sections (Smart Matching, Expiry Radar, etc.)
- High-quality food photography
- Warm, approachable color palette
- Clear CTAs for donor/recipient signup
