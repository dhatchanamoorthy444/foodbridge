FOODBRIDGE STICKER PACK

Contents:
- 00_full_sticker_sheet.png: original complete sticker sheet.
- 01_brand_and_donation: FoodBridge identity and donation/organization stickers.
- 02_recipient_and_community: recipient institution and community support stickers.
- 03_foodbridge_process_and_environment: process, food-waste, and sustainability stickers.
- 04_community_impact: community food-sharing illustrations.
- 05_dashboard_category_icons: small category icons for website dashboards.

Usage:
- Use the PNGs as visual assets in the FoodBridge website.
- These are cropped from one generated sticker sheet; some crops may include
  a little surrounding background or neighboring white sticker border.
- The illustrations are decorative and should not be treated as proof of
  organization verification or food safety.
- For production use, review text and crop edges before publishing.
